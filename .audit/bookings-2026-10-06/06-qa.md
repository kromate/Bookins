### Step 5 CSV detail
Bookings CSV: guest names/notes/phones beginning with `=`, `+` are prefixed with `'`. `=HYPERLINK("http://evil","x")` is exported as `'=HYPERLINK(""http://evil"",""x"")` with quotes escaped, so formula injection is neutralized. Side effect: legitimate phones starting with `+` get a literal apostrophe (`'+234 803 ...`) in both Bookings and Contacts CSV (B6). Filename follows the active tab (`bookings-upcoming-2026-10-06.csv`). Contacts CSV: same escaping.

## Bugs

### B1 (Low, preview-only in practice) Owner pages do not refresh after bookings made on /book in the same session
Repro: open /bookings (or Overview), navigate via router to /book#local-preview, create a booking, navigate back to /bookings. The new booking is missing (Upcoming 2 rather than 3) until "Refresh workspace" is pressed. Expected: lists reflect the new booking (or a visible stale-data hint). Actual: stale until manual refresh. Suspect: `/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/bookins/App.vue:185` (workspace loaded once on mount / on mode change) and no reload on route enter in `pages/Bookings.vue:121` (`onMounted(loadCalendarStatus)` only). In production /book is a separate guest session, so the realistic impact is that an owner who leaves Bookings open never sees new bookings without pressing Refresh; no polling or hint.

### B2 (Medium on mobile) Availability page is wider than the viewport at 375px
Repro: resize to 375px, open /availability. `.availability-main` / `.schedule-card` are about 384-403px wide; body and html are `overflow-x: hidden`, so the right edge (Remove-window buttons, day toggle area, select triggers right edges at 384px) is clipped by about 9-28px and cannot be scrolled to. Expected: fits 375px. Suspect: grid child missing `min-width: 0` at `pages/Availability.vue:432-433` (`.availability-main` / `.schedule-card`) and `.days` at `pages/Availability.vue:623` (mobile override at :786). Worse with multiple windows per day. Measured by DOM, not visually confirmed.

### B3 (Low) Settings version chip is stale
Settings shows "Bookins v0.3.0 candidate" while `package.json:4` and `.goalmatic/app.json:11` are 0.4.0 (and README documents v0.4.0 features). Suspect: `pages/Settings.vue:295` hard-coded string.

### B4 (Low, UX) Inverted date range gives no explanation
Bookings From=Oct 12, To=Oct 8 shows "No bookings match your filters" with no message that the range is inverted. Suspect: `pages/Bookings.vue` date filtering (~line 253 area).

### B5 (Low, cosmetic) Contacts display name/avatar
Contact name comes from the latest booking, so a guest entered once as "Race Guest" and later as "race guest" shows lowercased. A name starting with `=` renders "=" as the avatar initial. Contact list order is not obviously sorted (not by name, last, or next).

### B6 (Low) CSV formula guard alters valid phone numbers
Phones starting with `+` are exported with a leading apostrophe in Bookings and Contacts CSV (`'+234 803 555 0182`). Safe against injection but the apostrophe is visible in some tools and breaks lookups. Suspect: the CSV escape helper used by `pages/Bookings.vue:406-412` and `pages/Contacts.vue`. Consider guarding `+` and `-` only for non-phone columns, or documenting.

### B7 (Info) Local preview link token is low entropy
`createPublicLink` in local preview (`booking.js:346`) pads a 32-hex UUID with 11 trailing zeros to reach 43 characters. Preview-only, never hosted, but not random across all 43 chars.

### B8 (Info) Required description relies on native browser validation
The service form requires Description but shows no in-form message from Bookins; the browser's native tooltip is the only feedback. In my automation the Create click silently did nothing until I realized the field was empty. Real browsers show the tooltip. `pages/Services.vue` form.

### B9 (Info) Window end at 23:59 allows a 60-min service at 23:00
A 60-minute "Product consultation" at 23:00 is offered with a 23:59 window end, so it ends 00:00 (past the window). This matches the intended "interval slot appears" behavior, but the booking runs one minute beyond the configured window.
